# Contributing

Any violation of the following rules and guidelines may result in your issue/pull-request/discussion being closed (with this document being cited) and yourself being banned from interacting with this repository.

Please conduct yourself with respect. Do not be rude to anyone, including but not limited to any contributor, collaborator, reporter, developer, anyone mentioned in the guide either directly or by proxy, myself, and yourself. Do not dismiss the opinions others in favor of someone else's opinion.

Be respectful of people's time, do not pressure or constantly bug people to do something. Reminders are fine if someone said they would do something within a given time-frame, but keep them short and far between.

Don't bring issues from outside the scope of this guide/repository, unless it pertains to a browser listed on the selection page then it isn't relevant and will likely be ignored.

## Issues & Discussions

If you want a browser added, please use the issue template. Try to avoid a blank issues or discussions for this.

If you come across a bug, please open an issue. When filing an issue please include your local copy of the `ConfigGen.py` script, the `Configuration.config` file, and the output files from the `ConfigGen.py` script.

Keep issue titles simple and direct, use the description for details.

If you have a question, open a discussion thread. If you require support, open an issue.

Generative AI usage for issues and discussions is strictly forbidden for anything other than translation, usage must be disclosed if used. Generative AI may be quoted if, for example, it was used during research or was used to assist in investigating a bug.

## Pull Requests

Some requirements of your PRs:
- all code must be your own, do not submit some else's code without their permission
- all writing must be your own unless it is a quote, make sure to include citations if so
- all writing that contains claims or facts should have an appropriately cited source supporting said claim or fact
- all commits must be signed
- commit and PR titles should be semantic (i.e. prefixed with `feat:`, `chore:`, `docs:`, etc.)
- major changes should be documented, including intent

Generative AI usage is permitted for small changes, like white-spaces, typos, or small-scale formatting. Any other usage is forbidden. Any usage must be disclosed, including what model was used.
